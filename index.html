Wing Scale

Lepidopteran microstructure visualization

Morris Aguilar

Each of the ~4500 scales is a custom 3D mesh resembling lepidopteran wing microstructure. Structural coloration and iridescence are modeled through custom GLSL shaders and real-time Three.js rendering.
